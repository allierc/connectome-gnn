\textbf{What we revise.} $\tau$ survives: full-sample $0.99$ against the oracle's
$1.00$ at $\sigma = 0.05$; noise-free it does not ($-11.80$ against $0.09$).
$V^{\mathrm{rest}}$ does not: $0.76$ against $0.95$ at $\sigma = 0.05$, and
negative in every degraded row. We keep ``on par with an oracle'' for $W$ and
$\tau$ at $\sigma > 0$, drop it for $V^{\mathrm{rest}}$, and report the degraded
rows as not recovered.

\textbf{Criterion.} The excluded set must not be defined by the residual it
excuses, so we define it a priori: zero in-degree ($44$ neurons) and neurons
never crossing threshold, whose $V^{\mathrm{rest}}$ nothing constrains. We report
its size. With that mask fixed we call a parameter recovered at
full-sample $R^2 > 0.9$, partial at $0 < R^2 \leq 0.9$, not recovered at
$R^2 \leq 0$: $W$ and $\tau$ recovered for $\sigma \geq 0.05$,
$V^{\mathrm{rest}}$ recovered at $\sigma = 0.5$, partial at $0.05$, not recovered
when degraded.

Last, the Known-ODE runs were filtered at $\delta_{V} = 0.1$, not the stated
$0.2$, so their inlier $V^{\mathrm{rest}}$ is twice as tightly filtered as the
GNN's; full-sample is unaffected and we will recompute those columns at $0.2$.
