\paragraph{W9.}
\emph{$N$.} $13{,}741$ neurons, $13{,}697$ with at least one incoming edge and
$44$ with none. Appx.~C counts the former, so the discrepancy is neurons with no
\emph{input}, not no output. On the same graph
$\sum_i \max(0, d_i - 45) = 115{,}223$, matching Appx.~C exactly.

\emph{$\pm 0.00$.} Rounding, not under-dispersion. At four decimals the five
folds give $R^2_{\widehat W} = 0.9860, 0.9862, 0.9829, 0.9874, 0.9886$ for the
GNN at $\sigma = 0.05$ (SD $0.0021$), against SD $0.0002$ for Known-ODE. A rerun
of a byte-identical configuration differs by $0.0014$, so the GNN spread sits
near the floor set by non-deterministic GPU reduction, and the Known-ODE fit,
least squares in the true parameters, is an order of magnitude below it. We will
report three decimals so these rows read $\pm 0.002$.

The $0.90 \pm 0.21$ entry is one fold: noise-free GNN rollout $r$ is
$1.000, 1.000, 0.999, 0.482, 0.998$. Four folds roll out perfectly and one
diverges, which is what the noise-free degeneracy predicts and why we restrict
the claim to $\sigma > 0$ (Q3).

\paragraph{W5b.}
Agreed. We will add the FlyWire whole-brain LIF line, Shiu et al.\ and
BrainTrace, to Sec.~2 as the closest neighbour to this work: real activity,
whole-brain scale, fixed dynamical form. Appx.~C applies there directly. Weights
fitted to reproduce recorded activity on a fixed connectome are constrained only
outside the null space of the columnar repeat structure, so agreement with
activity does not establish that the fitted weights are the biological ones.

\paragraph{W4.}
Agreed, and it will not stay in Limitations. $R^2_{\widehat W} = 0.63$ (GNN) and
$0.69$ (Known-ODE) at $\gamma = 0.1$, $0.38$ and $0.42$ at $\gamma = 0.2$: the
oracle fails with us, so the obstacle is the inverse problem rather than our
estimator, but the practical consequence is the same. The contributions, not only
the Limitations, will state that the method is demonstrated under process noise
and is not yet usable at realistic measurement noise.

\paragraph{W7 and W8.}
Both abstract claims are overstated and we will soften them. The evidence for W7
is intrinsic noise in silico and the step to optogenetics is an untested analogy,
so the abstract will claim only that noise and perturbation improve
identifiability in simulation. For W8, the SIREN rollout is scored on
training-frame indices and the stimulus is largely present in the $1{,}736$
photoreceptor activities, so ``recovers unknown visual stimuli'' comes out of the
abstract and the result stays in the appendix with that qualification.
