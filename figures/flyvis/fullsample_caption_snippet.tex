For the per-neuron parameters, each cell reports the inlier $R^2$ (full-sample $R^2$) [excluded \%]: the inlier value is the identity-line $R^2$ (\cref{eq:r2_identity}) over the residual-filtered set (\cref{eq:outlier_threshold}), the parenthetical value is the same statistic over \emph{all} neurons, and the bracketed value is the percentage of neurons excluded by the filter. Full-sample $R^2$ can be negative and is reported as-is. Here $\hat{\tau}$ uses variant~(A), i.e.\ $\hat{\tau}_i=-1/s_i$ clipped to [0, 1]; the unclipped variant~(B) ($\hat{\tau}_i=-1/s_i$ with no bound) can be arbitrarily negative for degenerate slopes $s_i\to 0$ and is not tabulated. $R^2_{\hat{W}}$ and cluster accuracy are already unfiltered and are unchanged.
