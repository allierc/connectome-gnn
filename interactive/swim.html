The fish

Minimal live re-implementation of the dorsal-IPN HD ring (companion to the zebrafish manuscript). Swim impulses follow the swim-integration convention (≈ π/4 rad per turn). The fish's heading is read out from the bump by population vector — turning steers it, swimming forward moves it along that heading. Built with Claude · reviewed by C. Allier.
