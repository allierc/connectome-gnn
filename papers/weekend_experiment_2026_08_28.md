# Weekend experiment — 45 jobs, launched 2026-08-28

Two questions, six tasks, all on `gpu_l4`, ~33 h per job, one batch.

**Q1 (Task 1).** Does a rollout / recurrent training curriculum beat plain t+1 training on
`flyvis_A`, and if it decays with horizon, *which mechanism* causes the decay?

**Q2 (Tasks 2–6).** Can g_phi be made to depend only on the presynaptic inputs `(v_j, a_j)`,
and is the reported "noise probe helps" effect real?

---

## 1. Background

The task is an inverse problem: recover the connectome `W` from simulated neural dynamics
with a GNN. Headline metric is `R²_W`, the R² between learned and ground-truth `W`.

The data is generated by a model whose edge function depends **only** on presynaptic
`(v_j, a_j)`. Two model families are trained against it:

| family | g_phi inputs | first-layer width (emb_dim = 2) | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `flyvis_A` | `(v_j, a_j)` | 3 | **correctly specified** |
| `flyvis_conductance` | `(v_i, v_j, a_i, a_j)` | 6 | over-parameterised — `v_i`, `a_i` are not in the generative model |

The **noise probe** appends 2 pure `N(0,1)` columns to g_phi's input, redrawn every forward
pass. They are uninformative by construction, so they are a *known-zero calibration*: whatever
`∂g_phi/∂noise` settles at is what "fully discarded" looks like for this model, at this
regularisation strength, at this point in training. Without them, a `v_i` group norm of 0.3 is
uninterpretable.

### Prior results this builds on

| run | final `R²_W` | @iter |
|---|---|---|
| `nominal_grok_cv00` (flyvis_A, t+1) | 0.9752 | 1.59 M |
| `nominal_rollout_mean_grok_cv00` (K=1…10) | 0.9605 | 468 k |
| `conductance_grok_cv00` (seed 1041) | 0.6425 | 5.76 M |
| `conductance_cv00` (seed 1041) | **0.5582** | 1.52 M |
| `conductance_cv01` (seed 1042, **same data**) | **0.9334** | 1.52 M |
| `conductance_noiseprobe_grok_cv00` (seed 1041) | 0.9097 | 2.16 M |
| `grouplasso_{01,02,05,10}` | 0.549 / 0.594 / 0.711 / 0.616 | 6.32 M |

Two facts from this table drive the whole design:

1. **Seed 1041 is a difficult *seed*, not difficult *data*.** `conductance_cv00` and
   `conductance_cv01` point at the *same dataset* and differ only in training seed:
   0.558 vs 0.933. A seed change alone moves `R²_W` by **0.375**.
2. **The rollout curriculum peaks then decays.** `mean` reduction peaked 0.9826 at K=1→2
   (beating t+1's best-ever 0.9757, in 10× fewer iterations) then fell monotonically to
   0.9605 by K=10. `norm2` behaved identically in slope (−0.0213 vs −0.0215) but 0.012 lower
   throughout. So **the reduction sets the level and the horizon sets the slope.**

## 2. The tasks

### Task 1 — rollout strategies vs t+1 (8 jobs)
`flyvis_A`, cv00, seed 1042, `fit_reduction: mean`, 5 epochs, K = [1,2,3,4,5].

| job | arm | knob | steps | evals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| …360 | `onestep` | *no rollout* | 1,600,000 | 1,600,000 |
| …361 | `onestep_step_matched` | dal 46 | 736,000 | 736,000 |
| …362 | `uniform` | current scheme | 730,666 | 1,599,998 |
| …363 | `pushforward` | `bptt_window: 1` | 730,666 | 1,599,998 |
| …364 | `shoot2` | `shooting_stride: 2` | 730,666 | 1,599,998 |
| …365 | `shoot1` | `shooting_stride: 1` | 730,666 | 1,599,998 |
| …4xx | `discount` | `γ = 0.5` | 730,666 | 1,599,998 |
| …367 | `last` | endpoint-only | 730,666 | 1,599,998 |

**Expected insight.** The rollout arms take 2.2× fewer optimizer steps than `onestep` at equal
compute — `onestep_step_matched` separates "rollout helps" from "rollout takes fewer steps".
Then `pushforward` tests whether the horizon decay is the K-deep BPTT chain; `shoot2` whether
it is teacher-forcing dilution (only step 0 starts from an observation, so the clean supervised
fraction falls as 1/K); `discount` whether it is the drifted late steps' gradient share.
`shoot1` and `last` bound the axes: K independent one-step fits, and the legacy endpoint-only
objective.

### Task 2 — conductance noise probe × 5 folds × lasso (10 jobs)
Width 8, `norm2`, 1.6 M iterations. Folds cv00–cv04, seeds 1041–1045, each fold its **own**
dataset. Group lasso 2.0 (…368–372) vs 0 (…373–377), paired within fold.

### Task 3 — the same probe on the correctly-specified family (5 jobs, …413–417)
Width 5, group lasso 0. Verified field-by-field: the **only** differences from its Task 2
`nolasso` partner are `signal_model_name` and `input_size`. Zero training-config differences.

**Expected insight — this is the pivot of Q2.** On `flyvis_A` nothing is redundant, so the noise
columns are the *only* useless inputs. If `flyvis_A` drives `∂g_phi/∂noise → 0` and conductance
does not, the conductance result is about **optimisation**. If *neither* does, the whole discard
framing is about **credit assignment**, not about `v_i`/`a_i` being redundant.

### Task 4 / 4b — group-lasso dose response (4 + 5 jobs)
cv00, seed 1041. Task 4 sweeps lasso 5/10/20/50 **with** noise columns (width 8); Task 4b is the
width-6 twin at 2/5/10/20/50. With Task 2's cv00 arms this gives two 6-point curves
(0/2/5/10/20/50) differing only in whether the calibration instrument is attached.

### Task 5 — is the noise-probe gain repeatable? (10 jobs, …456–465)
Dataset cv00 **held fixed**, seeds 1041–1045 × noise ON/OFF, lasso 0 and every other coefficient
identical. **Only the noise columns and the seed vary.**

**Why this exists.** The claim "the noise probe improved results on the difficult seed 1041"
rests on `noiseprobe_grok` 0.910 @2.16 M vs `conductance_grok` 0.642 @5.76 M. Those two configs
differ in **four** ways at once — noise columns 0→2, `coeff_g_phi_weight_L1` 0.28→0, group lasso
0→2.0, and iteration count. And per fact (1) the seed-alone spread (0.375) **exceeds** the
claimed effect (0.27). n=1 cannot separate a real benefit from a lucky draw; a 5×2 paired grid can.

### Task 6 — g_phi_norm anchor control (2 jobs, added after audit)
`coeff_g_phi_norm: 0` for both families at cv00/1041. See §3.

## 3. Design audit — what a 77-agent adversarial review found

72 defects proposed, 48 refuted on the facts, 23 survived. The three that changed the grid:

### ❶ FIXED — Task 3 was writing no results at all
The metric functions were generalised to the `flyvis_A` layout, but the **call site** in
`graph_trainer.py` was still gated on `if 'flyvis_conductance' in signal_model_name`. Verified
against the live jobs: `noiseprobe_nolasso_cv00` had 7 rows of `g_phi_discard.log`,
`noiseprobe_flyvisA_cv00/01` had **no file**. All 5 Task-3 jobs would have trained 33 h and
produced nothing — silently, because an absent file is indistinguishable from "this model has no
such metric". Now gated on the layout. Fixed, relaunched, confirmed live
(`1,0.757162,nan,nan,2.214150` — cosine written, `vi`/`ai` correctly `nan`, `ratio_noise` = 2.21).

### ❷ NOT FIXED BY DESIGN — the g_phi_norm anchor is family-dependent
`regularizer.py:409` does `in_features_edge_norm[:, 0] = 2*xnorm`, hardcoding **column 0** —
which is `v_i` on conductance but `v_j` on `flyvis_A`. The two families therefore get genuinely
different priors, and Task 2 ↔ Task 3 is not strictly paired on that term.

**I deliberately did not patch this.** Re-pointing the anchor at `v_j` for conductance would
implicitly demand g_phi be flat in `v_i` — which *is* the discard hypothesis under test. The fix
would manufacture the result. Task 6 instead switches the prior off in both families, making that
one pair exactly comparable.

### ❸ RE-DOSED — `discount` at γ=0.9 was a null dose
At γ=0.9 over K≤5 the normalised per-step gradient shares are 0.244/0.220/0.198/0.178/0.160
against uniform's 0.200 — a maximum shift of **4.4 percentage points**, well below the ~0.007–0.010
`R²_W` effect it is meant to resolve. Relaunched at γ=0.5 (shares 0.516/0.258/0.129/0.065/0.032).

### Surviving caveats — analysis rules, not relaunches

- **Never read a single final checkpoint.** The reference runs contain one-checkpoint collapses
  of 0.2–0.5 in `R²_W` (`noiseprobe_grok`: 0.8985 → **0.3637** → 0.9104 across three consecutive
  rows). Report the median over the last ~320 k iterations.
- **1.6 M is enough after all.** My earlier "8× below the design point" caveat is **wrong**:
  `noiseprobe_grok` is flat from 800 k on — 0.9159 @800 k, 0.9192 @960 k, 0.9004 @1.6 M,
  0.9097 @2.16 M. No trend. The budget is fine for Tasks 2–5.
- **n=1 per cell in Tasks 1, 4, 4b.** Expected effects (0.007–0.022) sit at or below the known
  spread. Treat these as *screens*, not rankings; estimate σ from Task 5's 5 seeds and from the
  replicate pair below. Differences under ~0.010 are unresolved, not ordered.
- **`rs_shoot2` is nearly inert.** With stride 2 over K=[1,2,3,4,5] the anchor fires
  `floor((K−1)/2)` = 0,0,1,1,2 times — **4 anchor events in 730,666 steps** — and it is
  bit-identical to `rs_uniform` for 65.7% of them. It may be indistinguishable from `uniform`;
  that is not evidence shooting doesn't work. `shoot1` carries that axis.
- **The cosine score is not comparable across families.** Its denominator has 3 non-zero groups
  on `flyvis_A` vs 5 on conductance, so part of any gap is arithmetic. Use `ratio_noise`
  (column 4, defined identically in both) as the cross-family headline.
- **41 design cells, not 45.** `noiseprobe_nolasso_cv00` and `noiseseed_on_s1041` are
  config-identical. Since `deterministic: false`, they are a free **replicate pair** measuring the
  run-to-run floor — keep both, count them once.
- **Task 1 has no `norm2` bridge.** All 8 arms use `mean` with coefficients divided by 70,467, so
  `rs_onestep` is *not* the historical 0.9752 baseline. Internally consistent; do not lay these
  numbers on the old ones.
- **Epoch 0 is shared by all 6 rollout arms** (bit-identical, verified: 0.950013 at iter 16,001).
  That is 44% of each arm's steps. It is common-random-numbers pairing, not waste — every arm
  branches from one checkpoint, so post-branch differences are attributable to the treatment
  alone. But it means the arms only differentiate over the last 410 k steps.

## 4. What we expect to learn

| question | decided by | outcome that would settle it |
|---|---|---|
| Does rollout beat t+1 at equal compute? | T1 `uniform` vs `onestep` vs `onestep_step_matched` | if `step_matched` ≈ rollout, the gap is step count and rollout is neutral |
| Why does `R²_W` decay with horizon? | T1 `pushforward` / `shoot2` / `discount` | whichever arm stays flat across K names the mechanism |
| Does unrolling add anything over more frames? | T1 `shoot1` | if `shoot1` ≈ rollout arms, unrolling contributes nothing |
| Can g_phi discard a provably useless input? | T3 `ratio_noise` on `flyvis_A` | `→0` means yes, when the family is correct |
| Is `v_i`/`a_i` retention redundancy or failed credit assignment? | T3 vs T2 | if `flyvis_A` discards noise but conductance doesn't, it's optimisation |
| Does the group lasso force the discard? | T4 + T4b dose curves | monotone `ratio_noise`/`ratio_vi` decline vs an `R²_W` cost |
| Do the noise columns perturb the lasso? | T4 vs T4b at matched dose | difference-of-differences (n=1 — screen only) |
| **Is the noise-probe gain real or a seed lottery?** | T5, 5 seeds paired | paired mean ± sd of (ON − OFF) over 5 seeds |
| Is the family comparison prior-confounded? | T6 | if T6 reproduces T2↔T3, the anchor asymmetry didn't matter |

**The single most likely headline:** Task 5. It is the only cell in the grid with enough
replication to make a claim, and it targets a result the group already believes. Early signal at
iteration 3,201 on seed 1041 is **noise ON 0.371 vs OFF −0.043**, consistent with the claim — but
that is one seed and one early checkpoint, which is exactly the evidence standard Task 5 exists
to replace.
